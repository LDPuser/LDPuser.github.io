I am a recent graduate in Data Science and Statistics from UCLouvain, with a particular interest in machine learning applied to omics data.

Since July 2026, I have been a [Junior Orsi Fellow](https://juniororsifellowships.framer.website/alumni), a Belgian fellowship that brought me into the Harvard–MIT research ecosystem as a research scholar in the [Abudayyeh–Gootenberg Lab](https://www.abugootlab.org/), where I contribute to a project on AI Virtual Cell (AIVC) modeling.

Building an AI virtual cell, or even getting closer to one, is to me one of the most exciting scientific endeavours in computational biology today. Two facets of it drive me. The first is **understanding**: modelling how a cell behaves and evolves, especially under external stress, in order to infer regulatory mechanisms, uncover how genes interact, and interrogate the effects of drugs. The second, of the upmost importance, is **applying**: using AIVC candidates to screen pharmaceutical hypotheses in silico, as part of an active-learning loop with the wet lab. It is a project as ambitious as it is exciting, and one that requires working across modalities, disciplines, and scales.
