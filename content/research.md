### Multimodal molecular representations for virtual cell modelling
*Harvard Medical School, Abudayyeh–Gootenberg Lab · 2026–present*

Most models of cellular perturbation treat drugs as categorical labels, which makes them unable to predict responses to compounds never seen during training. Building on a harmonized compound vocabulary across large-scale single-cell perturbation atlases, I am working on multimodal molecular representation learning. Ultimately, the goal is to provide biologically informed embeddings that can serve as general-purpose conditioning for foundation models aiming to generalize to unseen drugs.

### Continuous-time generative modelling of glycemic responses
*M.Sc. thesis, UCLouvain · 2025–2026 · advised by Laura Symul*

I introduced CLODE, a controlled latent ODE-RNN in which meals act as exogenous inputs on continuous-time latent dynamics. On an *in silico* population, the model enables personalized forecasting of glucose trajectories and counterfactual simulation of dietary interventions.

### Statistical pre-processing of glycomics data
*SMCS, UCLouvain · 2025–2026 · with collaborators at UCLouvain and MIT*

I contributed to the development of  `glycomix`, an R package that turns raw CE-LIF chromatography traces into glycan feature tables. In particular, I was in charge of preprocessing the raw chromatograms, performing denoising, artefact removal, and temporal alignment. The work was accepted as a poster at EuroBioC 2026.
